THE CLOCKWORK BEHEMOTH: BOSS ENCOUNTER
​Phase 0: The Summoning
The boss is triggered by a specific button at set coordinates. Once pressed, the button vanishes. The Behemoth spawns 10 blocks underground and rises to the surface over 5 seconds. During this time, the boss is invulnerable, the "assemble" animation plays, and the brass-themed boss bar fills from 0 to 500 HP in sync with its rising height.
​Phase 1: Stable Machine (500 HP to 125 HP)
The boss uses a Decision Engine to choose a special move every 4 seconds. Its standard melee is an alternating Left Hook and Right Hook.
​Special 1: Earthquake Slam. The "slam" animation plays, causing AOE damage and ground cracks.
​Special 2: Drone Deployment. The "deploy" animation plays, spawning 3 Gear Drones. These drones use Vex AI to hunt players and have a 50% chance to drop Andesite Alloy if killed.
​Special 3: Steam Repulsion. A 1-second warning hiss plays, followed by the "steam_vent" animation which blasts all nearby players back.
​Mechanic: All active drones automatically self-destruct 1 second before the boss starts its next special move.
​Phase 2: Critical State (Below 125 HP)
The boss enters an overheat mode. The boss bar name changes to "CRITICAL STATE" in red. Its movement speed increases by 50%, the special move timer is cut in half, and all melee attacks now set players on fire. Visual fire and steam particles vent constantly from its back.
​Phase 3: Shutdown and Loot
Upon reaching 0 HP, the boss stops and plays a dedicated "death" animation. Cinematic explosions trigger around it (visual only, no damage).
​Guaranteed Drops: 1 Clockwork Core, 1 Behemoth Conqueror Trophy, 3 Precision Mechanisms, 3 Brass Blocks, and 2 Andesite Alloy Blocks.
​Cooldown: A 10-minute server timer starts. The button will not reappear and the boss cannot be spawned again until this timer hits zero.
